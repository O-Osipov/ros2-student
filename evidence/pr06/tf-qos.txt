$ timeout 4s ros2 topic echo /tf_static --once --qos-durability volatile --qos-reliability reliable
exit=124


The fixed sensor transforms are published once with reliable, transient_local QoS. A late transient_local reader receives the retained sample; a late volatile reader receives no historical sample. Dynamic /tf uses volatile QoS because its transforms change over time.
